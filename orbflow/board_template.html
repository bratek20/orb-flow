How to read this

Each row is a full round: 1 div → (chaos/ex) → item → (chaos/ex) → div, with gold paid on every leg (gold = units you WANT × that item's fee). stable = share of hours where the sell price was at least the min gap above the buy price; one lucky hour does not count. Prices are trades from past hours, not the live book. Check the live ratio in game before you place an order. gold? = fee unknown, guessed at %%DEFAULT_GOLD%%/unit. proven = you have traded it. The ▸ button shows the hourly history. The planner splits your stash greedily: cheapest gold per div of profit first, at most what each market trades in the hours you can wait, one route per item. It is a heuristic; check the live book in game.
